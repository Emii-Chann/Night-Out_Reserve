Privacy Policy & Cookie Usage

Effective Date: April 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Night-Out Reserve. We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use, and share your data when you visit our website and use our booking services.

2. Information We Collect

To provide our services efficiently, we collect the following information:

  • Personal Details: Your name, email address, and phone number when you register an account.
  • Booking Data: Information about the venues, tables, or games you book, including dates, times, and party sizes.
  • Cookies: Small data files stored on your browser to keep you logged in and to remember your preferences (like the cookie consent itself).

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your data exclusively to:

  • Create and manage your user account.
  • Process and confirm your reservations with the respective venues.
  • Communicate with you regarding your bookings (e.g., cancellations or modifications).
  • Ensure the security of our platform.

4. Information Sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties. However, when you make a booking, your relevant details (Name, Phone number, and Booking specifics) are shared with the Venue Administrators so they can prepare for your arrival.

5. Your GDPR Rights

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), you have the right to:

  • Access: View the data we hold about you (available on your Profile page).
  • Update: Correct any inaccurate information.
  • Delete: Request the deletion of your account and associated personal data.

6. Contact Us

If you have questions or comments about this Privacy Policy, please contact the site administrator or reach out to us directly through the platform.
